Firefly Shepherd (فائر فلائی شیفرڈ) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026
Developer: Global Hosting Service, Rawalpindi, Pakistan
Contact: me@arslan.pk

1. What Firefly Shepherd is

Firefly Shepherd is a firefly-guiding puzzle game. Your levels, stars, coins and upgrades and settings are stored only on your own device. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number or any contact details.

2. Weekly league (optional)

The game has an optional weekly league. To show the leaderboard, the app creates an anonymous player ID (using Google Firebase) and uploads only your weekly points and the display name you choose yourself. This display name can be anything — it does not have to be your real name — and it is visible to other players on the leaderboard. No other personal information is collected. League data is stored with Google Firebase (Firestore) under Google's privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. If you never open the league screen and never play while online, no league data is created.

3. Advertising

The free game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data, as described in Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent, and you can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.

4. In-app purchases (optional)

The game offers optional purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account so they can be restored, and the coins or ad-free status are stored on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children

Firefly Shepherd is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all progress from your phone and disconnects the anonymous league ID. To have your league scores and display name removed from the leaderboard, email us at me@arslan.pk with your display name and we will delete them.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: me@arslan.pk
