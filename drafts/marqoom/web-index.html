What Marqoom does

Entry by voice or typing

Speak a sentence and the name, amount and gave/got are filled in. Or type, with a calculator inside the amount box.

Khata for every customer

Customers, suppliers and personal khatas with running balance, due dates, customer photos and receipt photos.

WhatsApp reminders

Send the pending balance to a customer in one tap, with your JazzCash, bank or wallet details included.

Bills and statements

Make a bill with items and share it as PDF or image. Share a full statement as image, PDF or Excel file.

Cash book and committee

Daily income and expenses with a monthly summary, and a committee (BC) tracker for monthly instalments.

Private and offline

No sign-up and no OTP. Your data stays on your phone, with PIN or fingerprint lock and backups.

How to use it

  1. Add a khata. Tap "New khata" and type the customer's name, or pick them from your contacts.
  2. Record money. Tap "You gave" or "You got", or press the mic and say it.
  3. Remind and share. Tap "Remind" to send the balance on WhatsApp, or share the statement.
  4. Stay safe. Set a PIN and save a backup file from time to time.

Questions

Is Marqoom free?

Yes. It shows one small banner ad and never shows full-screen ads. You can remove the ad with a one-time purchase.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Adding entries, balances and statements all work offline. Internet is needed only to send a WhatsApp message.

Where is my data stored?

On your own phone. There is no sign-up and no server. You can save a backup file, and Android's Google backup can restore your khata on a new phone.

Can I move my customers from another khata app?

Yes. Export them as an Excel/CSV file with name, phone and balance, then use "Import from Excel / CSV" in Marqoom.

Which languages are supported?

English, Urdu, Hindi, Bengali, Arabic, Punjabi and Pashto, including voice entry.
