Motorway Run (موٹروے رن) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026
Developer: Global Hosting Service, Rawalpindi, Pakistan
Contact: info@globalhostingservice.com

1. What Motorway Run is

Motorway Run (formerly Motorway Night Run) is a motorway racing game. Your progress (best distance, coins, cars, upgrades, career stars, missions and settings) is stored only on your own device and in Android's own device backup. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email, phone number or any contact details.

2. Online races and the weekly league

The game uses Google Firebase for its online parts. The first time the game goes online for you (when you start an online race or a race with a friend, open the weekly league, or finish a run that beats your best of the week while your phone is online), the app creates an anonymous player ID with Firebase Authentication. It does not need your real name, email or phone number for this. A short form of this ID is shown in the game's Settings as your player ID (for example 7Fq2-ZkL9).

Online races (against a random driver or a friend with a 6-digit room code): during a race the app sends to the other driver only what the race needs: the display name you chose in the game (it does not have to be your real name), which of the game's cars you drive, your number of online wins, the game version, and your car's position, speed and race events (such as a crash or the finish) several times a second. This race data is stored briefly in Google Firebase (Realtime Database): it stays while you and your rival are in the match (including rematches) and is removed within seconds after you leave it; data from a match or a friend's room that broke off (for example when a phone lost its connection) is cleared out after about an hour. To keep both phones' race clocks in step, the app briefly writes a time value and deletes it at once. The online features do not collect your location, contacts or device identifiers. There is no chat. If you report another player's name, your email app opens a message to us with that player's ID and name; we use it only to review the name.

Weekly league: when you finish a run that beats your best of the week while your phone is online, and after each online race, your points are sent to that week's league table in Google Firebase (Realtime Database), together with your display name (or “Player” if you have not chosen one) and the time of the update. This happens even if you never open the league screen. Other players can see your display name and points on the table. League tables are kept so that past weeks' results can be shown; we delete your entries when you ask us to (see section 6).

No other personal information is collected. Like any online service, Firebase receives your phone's IP address when the game connects to it. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Advertising

The game has no banner ads and no pop-up ads. A video ad is shown only when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching it (a second chance after a crash, or double coins). These videos are served by Google AdMob; to show and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data, as described here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent, and you can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.

4. In-app purchases (optional)

The game offers optional purchases (coin packs) through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. The coins you buy are stored on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children

Motorway Run is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all progress from your phone and disconnects the anonymous player ID. Online race data is removed when you leave a match.

To have your data in Motorway Run deleted:

  1. Email info@globalhostingservice.com with the subject “Delete my Motorway Run data”.
  2. Write your player ID, which you find in the game's Settings (for example 7Fq2-ZkL9), and the display name you use in the game.

Within 30 days we delete all your entries on the weekly league tables (display name, points and update times), and the anonymous player ID that was created for you in Firebase, and we reply to confirm. We keep no copy. Progress stored on your phone is removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. For any other request, email us at info@globalhostingservice.com.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@globalhostingservice.com
