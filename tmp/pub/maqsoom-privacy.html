Maqsoom (مقسوم) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026
Developer: Global Hosting Service
Contact: info@globalhostingservice.com
App page: globalhostingservice.com/apps/maqsoom/

1. Your data stays on your phone

Maqsoom is an app for splitting shared expenses. There is no sign-up and no account. Your groups, member names and phone numbers, expenses, payments, meal counts and receipt photos are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to any server of ours. We cannot see them.

2. Sharing is always your choice

The app sends something only when you choose to share it: a summary picture, a text summary, a PDF, a spreadsheet (CSV), a member's statement, a reminder or a group file. It then opens the app you pick (for example WhatsApp) with the message ready, and you press send yourself. Nothing is ever sent automatically. The optional "Send to group" step after a new expense is off unless you turn it on, and even then it only opens the chat for you to send.

3. Microphone and voice typing

The microphone is used only when you tap the Speak button to say an expense. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (on most phones this is Google's). The audio goes to that service to be converted; the app receives only the text. The app does not record or keep audio and never listens in the background.

4. Contacts

When you choose a group member from your contacts, Android's own contact picker opens and the app receives only the one contact you pick (name and phone number). The app has no permission to read your address book and never uploads your contacts.

5. Photos

Receipt photos you add are kept inside the app on your phone. They leave the phone only if you share them or include them in a backup.

6. PIN and fingerprint lock

If you set a PIN, only a scrambled (hashed) form of it is stored on your phone. Fingerprint unlock is handled by Android; the app never sees or stores your fingerprint.

7. Backup

A backup file is created only when you ask for it, and it goes only where you choose to save or send it. Separately, if Android's own backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data (groups and expenses) in your own Google account so that it comes back on a new phone. That backup is controlled by you in your phone's settings and is covered by Google's privacy policy. We cannot see it.

8. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a small banner ad at the bottom of the screen. It shows no full-screen ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use the device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers, the IP address (which gives an approximate location such as city or country), information about how the app is used (for example app starts and taps on ads) and diagnostic information. This data goes from the SDK to Google; we do not receive it and cannot link it to your name.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your phone's settings under Google › Ads. Read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), Google's consent message is shown before personalised ads are used.

9. Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Deleting your data

You can delete expenses, members and whole groups inside the app at any time. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app, removes everything the app stored on the phone. Because we hold none of your data on our side, there is nothing for us to delete. For data handled by Google's ad service, use the Google controls described in the Ads section.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: info@globalhostingservice.com
