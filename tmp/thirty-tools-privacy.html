Privacy policy

Thirty Tools for Android · Last updated 21 November 2026

Thirty Tools is a set of thirty everyday tools for photos, videos, numbers and text. It is published by Global Hosting Service. This page explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your photos, videos, text and numbers are processed on your phone. The app does not upload them to us or to anyone else.
  • You do not need an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • The app shows banner advertisements from Google AdMob. Google collects some technical information to show and measure these ads. Details are below.

Photos, videos and text you work on

You choose each file yourself with Android’s own picker, or by sharing it to Thirty Tools from another app. The app does not ask for permission to read your whole gallery, your camera, your microphone, your contacts or your location.

All resizing, compressing, converting, cropping, trimming and calculating happens on your device. While it works, the app may keep temporary copies in its private storage. They are removed when you finish, and any that remain are cleared the next time the app starts.

Files you save or share

When you tap Save, the result is stored on your phone in “Pictures/Thirty Tools”, “Movies/Thirty Tools” or “Download/Thirty Tools”, or in the place you choose. When you tap Share, the file goes to the app you pick, which handles it under its own privacy policy.

What the app remembers

Your favorite tools, recently used tools, theme, your note in Quick notes and the last watermark text are stored on your phone only. If Android backup is switched on for your phone, this small amount of app data is included in your own device backup, so that it can come back when you move to a new phone. You can erase it at any time in Settings → Clear saved data, or by uninstalling the app. Generated passwords and the numbers you type into calculators are not stored.

Advertising

Thirty Tools shows banner ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location such as country or city;
  • information about how you interact with the ads and the app, and basic diagnostic information.

We never give your photos, videos, notes or text to any advertising service. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your choice before personalised ads are used, and you can change that choice in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s settings. You can read how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Google Play services

The app uses Google Play to check whether a newer version is available and, occasionally, to ask whether you would like to rate it. Google Play handles this under Google’s privacy policy.

Children

Thirty Tools is a general utility app. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

We hold no account and no copy of your content, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Everything the app stores is on your phone, and you can remove it with Settings → Clear saved data or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
