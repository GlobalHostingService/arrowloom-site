Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-16

Mulsaq (“the app”) is made by Global Hosting Service. This page explains what happens to information when you use the app. Contact: info@globalhostingservice.com.

Your photos and stickers stay on your phone

You choose photos with Android's own photo picker. The app opens only the photo you pick and never asks for access to your gallery, camera, contacts or location.

Background removal runs on your phone with Google ML Kit. Your photo is not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Google's ML Kit software may send Google anonymous information about how it performs (for example the phone model, Android version and app version) so that Google can keep it working well; it does not send your photos.

The stickers and packs you make are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not have servers that receive them. If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the app's files in your own backup, under Google's control.

WhatsApp

When you tap “Add to WhatsApp”, WhatsApp reads that sticker pack (its name, pictures and emoji tags) from the app on your phone through WhatsApp's official sticker system. Each pack also carries this app's Google Play link, so people who receive your stickers can find the app. Stickers you send in WhatsApp are handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. Mulsaq is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

Sharing

When you share a sticker, the picture goes only to the app you choose in Android's share menu.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner on some screens, and a video ad only when you choose to watch one to unlock a ready-made pack. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (and an approximate location from it), and information about the ads you see and tap. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first; you can change your choice in Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

What we collect

We, Global Hosting Service, do not collect, store or sell any personal information from the app. The information described above is handled by Google (AdMob, ML Kit, Google Play) under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a sticker or a pack in the app, or uninstall the app, and it is gone from your phone. Packs already added to WhatsApp can be removed in WhatsApp's sticker settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
