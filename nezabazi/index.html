Tent Pegging League: Neza Bazi (نیزہ بازی) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026
Developer: Global Hosting Service, Rawalpindi, Pakistan
Contact: info@globalhostingservice.com

1. What the game is

Tent Pegging League: Neza Bazi (“the game”) is a tent-pegging game: you ride a horse and try to pierce and carry a peg with a lance. Your progress (coins, gold, horses, training, career stars and settings) is stored only on your own device and in Android's own device backup. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email, phone number or any contact details.

2. Online matches, the weekly league and the daily table

The game uses Google Firebase for its online parts. The first time you use one of them (an online match, a finished match or daily challenge that counts for the league, or opening the league tables), the app creates an anonymous player ID with Firebase Authentication. It does not need your real name, email or phone number for this.

Online matches (against a random rider or a friend with a room code): during a match the app sends to the other rider only what the match needs: the rider name and club name you chose in the game (they do not have to be your real name), your club colour, your horse and its training level, and your horse's position and results while you ride. This match data is stored briefly in Google Firebase (Realtime Database) and removed after the match.

Weekly league and daily table: when you finish a match (online or against a computer rider) or the daily challenge, the app saves your points on that week's league table and, for the daily challenge, on that day's table, in Google Cloud Firestore, together with the rider name and club name you chose and the time of the update. Other players can see your rider name, club and points on these tables. Past tables are kept so that results can be shown; to have your entries removed, email us your rider name and club.

No other personal information is collected. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

3. Advertising

The game has no banner ads and no pop-up ads. A video ad is shown only when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching it (for example double coins after a match). These videos are served by Google AdMob; to show and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data, as described here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent, and you can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.

4. In-app purchases (optional)

The game offers optional purchases (a one-time Starter Pack, coin packs and gold packs) through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account so the Starter Pack can be restored, and what you bought is stored on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children

The game is for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all progress from your phone and disconnects the anonymous player ID. Online match data is removed after each match.

To have your data in Tent Pegging League: Neza Bazi deleted:

  1. Email info@globalhostingservice.com with the subject “Delete my Neza Bazi data”.
  2. Write the rider name and club name you use in the game (you can see them in the game under Settings → Rider and club).

Within 30 days we delete all your entries on the weekly league and daily tables (rider name, club name, points and update times) and the anonymous player ID that was created for you in Firebase, and we reply to confirm. We keep no copy. Progress stored on your phone is removed by uninstalling the game or clearing its data. For any other request, email us at info@globalhostingservice.com.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@globalhostingservice.com
