Neza Bazi (نیزہ بازی) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026
Developer: Global Hosting Service, Rawalpindi, Pakistan
Contact: info@globalhostingservice.com

1. What Neza Bazi is

Neza Bazi is a tent-pegging game: you ride a horse and try to pierce and carry a peg with a lance. Your progress (coins, gold, horses, training, career stars and settings) is stored only on your own device and in Android's own device backup. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your real name, email, phone number or any contact details.

2. Online matches (optional)

When you choose to play online (against a random rider or a friend with a room code), the app creates an anonymous player ID with Google Firebase. During a match it sends to the other rider only what the match needs: the rider name and club name you chose in the game (they do not have to be your real name), your club colour, your horse and its training level, and your horse's position and results while you ride. This match data is stored briefly in Google Firebase (Realtime Database) and removed after the match. No other personal information is collected. Firebase's privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. If you never play online, no online data is created.

3. Advertising

The game has no banner ads and no pop-up ads. A video ad is shown only when you tap a button that offers a reward for watching it (for example double coins after a match). These videos are served by Google AdMob; to show and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device information such as the advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data, as described here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you are asked for consent, and you can reset or limit ad personalisation in your Android settings.

4. In-app purchases (optional)

The game offers optional purchases (a one-time Starter Pack, coin packs and gold packs) through Google Play Billing. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details. Purchases are linked to your Google Play account so the Starter Pack can be restored, and what you bought is stored on your device. Google's privacy policy applies to the payment: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Children

Neza Bazi is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

6. Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android settings, deletes all progress from your phone and disconnects the anonymous player ID. Online match data is removed after each match. For any other request, email us at info@globalhostingservice.com.

7. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: info@globalhostingservice.com
